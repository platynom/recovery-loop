# Commercial model

## Buyer

Subscription merchants, fintech operations teams, and SaaS finance leaders processing enough failed renewals that retry waste and involuntary churn are measurable.

## Value proposition

Recovery Loop increases recovered revenue per retry attempt while protecting customers and payment rails from low-value retries during outages, issuer stops, and exhausted budgets.

## Pricing hypothesis

- **Starter — ₹15,000/month:** up to 10,000 failed renewals, dashboard, basic policies, and monthly evidence report.
- **Growth — ₹49,000/month:** up to 100,000 failures, configurable budgets, bank-health automation, and API access.
- **Enterprise — custom:** higher volume, merchant-specific calibration, audit exports, SSO, support, and controlled experimentation.
- Optional performance fee only on independently measured incremental recovery above the merchant’s agreed fixed-ladder baseline.

## Proof required before selling

- At least one design partner using historical or test-mode data.
- A pre-registered baseline and fixed attempt budget.
- Calibration and false-refusal review by payment method and bank.
- Clear evidence that the product does not override issuer stop signals.
- Security and privacy review before any real merchant event data is processed.

## Resume-ready framing

“Built a refusal-aware payment recovery platform for Indian subscription payments with signed Razorpay webhooks, outage-aware policy gates, expected-value retry scheduling, D1 audit persistence, deterministic simulation, fixed-budget baseline evaluation, and a production-quality operations dashboard.”
